# Journey Clinical

> Journey Clinical is a telehealth platform for ketamine-assisted psychotherapy (KAP).
> It pairs licensed prescribers who handle medical eligibility, prescribing, and monitoring
> with a member network of independent psychotherapists who deliver preparation, dosing, and
> integration therapy. The platform also offers standalone medication management and Spravato
> (esketamine), operates across 25+ US states, and accepts major insurance plus HSA/FSA. It is
> a HIPAA-covered telehealth provider and does not publish a public developer API.
